## Overview

These are Harmonia's network-level KPIs — the indicators that tell us whether the trust network is actually working. They are designed to measure genuine collaboration and accountability, not vanity metrics like follower counts or event attendance.

All KPIs are tracked at chapter level and aggregated to network level. Chapter Coordinators are responsible for data collection; the Cross-Chapter Council reviews quarterly.

> [!important] Reporting deadline
> Member annual impact reports are due **31 March** each year. KPIs 3 and 8 cannot be calculated until reports are submitted.

---

## Network-Level KPIs

| # | KPI | Definition | Measurement Method | Frequency | Year 1 Target |
|---|-----|------------|-------------------|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | **Active members** | Organisations that have signed pledges and remain in good standing (not suspended) | Platform data + chapter records | Quarterly | 15–25 total (7–12 per chapter) |
| 2 | **Cross-sector partnerships formed** | Documented collaborations between ≥2 member organisations from different sectors, with a named joint activity | Self-report via platform, verified by Chapter Coordinator | Quarterly | 10 network-wide |
| 3 | **Pledge compliance rate** | % of members who: (a) submit annual report on time, (b) participate in ≥1 cross-sector initiative, (c) engage with peer accountability circle | Chapter annual review, cross-referenced with platform data | Annually | 80% |
| 4 | **Resources mobilised through the network** | Estimated value of financial, in-kind, and expertise contributions shared between members via Harmonia connections (not prior relationships) | Self-report in annual impact report | Annually | Baseline year — track, no target |
| 5 | **Sector balance** | % split of active members across NGO/Charity, Corporate, Startup, Philanthropist — tracked per chapter | Platform data | Quarterly | NGO 35–45%, Corporate 20–30%, Startup 20–30%, Philanthropist 10–20% |
| 6 | **Pilot projects underway** | Cross-sector projects with ≥2 member organisations formally engaged, documented in the platform | Committee tracking | Quarterly | 2 (at least 1 per chapter) |
| 7 | **Member retention rate** | % of founding members who renew membership at end of Year 1 | Chapter records | Annually | 85% |
| 8 | **Annual report submission rate** | % of members submitting their annual impact report by the 31 March deadline | Chapter Coordinator tracking | Annually | 90% |
| 9 | **Cross-chapter partnerships** | Documented partnerships between a Kenya chapter member and a UK chapter member | Platform data + Chapter Coordinator verification | Quarterly | 3 by end of Year 1 |
| 10 | **Network financial sustainability** | Membership fee revenue as % of total operating costs | Financial report | Annually | 60% by end of Year 1 |

## What These KPIs Don't Measure (and Why)

**Beneficiaries reached** — Harmonia is an infrastructure network, not a direct delivery organisation. Beneficiary reach is a downstream outcome tracked by member organisations, not the network itself. We track what we can control: the quality and depth of collaboration.

**Social media reach / PR** — not a signal of network health. A trust network with 20 deeply committed members is more valuable than one with 200 passive affiliates and a strong Twitter following.

**Number of events held** — attendance is not collaboration. We measure partnerships formed and resources shared, not bodies in rooms.

## Chapter-Level Breakdown

Each chapter tracks the same KPIs independently, then reports to the Cross-Chapter Council. This allows comparison between chapters and early identification of chapter-specific issues.

| KPI | Kenya Chapter Target (Year 1) | UK Chapter Target (Year 1) |
|-----|-------------------------------|---------------------------|
| Active members | 7–12 | 7–12 |
| Cross-sector partnerships | 5 | 5 |
| Pledge compliance rate | 80% | 80% |
| Pilot projects | 1 | 1 |
| Member retention | 85% | 85% |
| Annual report submission | 90% | 90% |

## Adding Program-Level KPIs

When specific programs or initiatives launch (Innovation Hub, cross-sector campaigns, etc.), they get their own impact framework with program-specific KPIs. Those feed into this network-level view as aggregate indicators under KPI 2 (partnerships) and KPI 6 (pilot projects).

Use the `impact-builder` skill to generate program-level frameworks.
