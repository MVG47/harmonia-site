## Purpose

This document defines what members submit in their annual impact report and how the network compiles those into a network-level impact summary. Consistent reporting is what makes Harmonia's collective impact measurable — and what gives the network credibility with funders and partners.

---

## Member Annual Impact Report

**Due:** 31 March each year (for the preceding calendar year)
**Submitted to:** Chapter Coordinator
**Format:** Complete the template below and submit via the Unified Impact Platform or email to your Chapter Coordinator

## Network Annual Impact Report

The Cross-Chapter Council publishes a network-level annual impact report each year, compiled from:
- Member annual reports (aggregated and anonymised where appropriate)
- Platform data (KPI 1–10 from [[KPIs-and-Metrics]])
- Chapter Coordinator observations
- External evaluation findings (every 3 years)

**Structure of the network annual report:**

1. Network snapshot (member count, sector balance, chapter breakdown)
2. KPI scorecard (Year N vs. Year N-1)
3. Collaboration highlights (2–3 case studies of cross-sector partnerships)
4. Accountability summary (pledge compliance rate, any Level 3+ cases in aggregate)
5. Financial summary (revenue, costs, sustainability ratio)
6. Lessons learned and priorities for next year
7. Chapter highlights (Kenya and UK separately)
