## What Harmonia Does That None of These Do

1. **Binding cross-sector pledges with graduated accountability** — no comparable network combines cross-sector membership with enforceable commitments
2. **Co-equal chapter governance from day one** — Kenya and UK chapters have equal authority; no probationary period, no Northern hierarchy
3. **Built-in trust infrastructure** — the platform, peer accountability circles, and shared measurement framework work together to make collaboration verifiable, not just claimed
