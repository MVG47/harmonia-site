## Vision

> A world where collaboration is the catalyst for transformative change — a harmonious and sustainable future for all, where the collective impact of compassionate efforts far exceeds the sum of individual contributions.

## Mission

> To build a trust network that unites NGOs, philanthropists, charities, corporates, and startups through shared principles and pledges, enabling them to collaborate, share resources, and amplify their collective impact for social good.

---

## The Problem We Solve

The global social impact sector has extraordinary people and resources — but they are fragmented, siloed, and working at a fraction of their collective potential.

| Problem | What it looks like |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Fragmentation** | Thousands of organisations independently pursuing similar goals, duplicating efforts, competing for the same grants |
| **Trust deficit** | No mechanism to verify whether organisations actually follow through on commitments |
| **Sector silos** | NGOs, corporates, and startups have almost no structured pathways for meaningful collaboration |
| **Resource inefficiency** | Funding, expertise, and networks are scattered — the right partners rarely find each other |
| **Accountability gaps** | Voluntary commitments without enforcement produce good intentions, not sustained action |

**The result:** The whole is far less than the sum of its parts.

Harmonia exists to fix this by building the trust-based infrastructure, platform, and culture that collaborative social impact requires.

---

## Core Principles

All Harmonia members — regardless of sector, size, or geography — are bound by six principles:

| Principle | What it means in practice |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| **Transparency** | Open sharing of activities, outcomes, and challenges with fellow members — not just the successes |
| **Accountability** | Commitment to measurable goals and honest reporting; graduated consequences for those who don't follow through |
| **Collaboration** | Active participation in shared initiatives; willingness to support other members, not just extract value |
| **Equity** | Fair treatment for all partners regardless of size; genuine voice for grassroots organisations alongside large institutions |
| **Impact First** | Prioritising genuine social outcomes over organisational self-interest or reputational benefit |
| **Continuous Improvement** | Commitment to learning, adapting, and sharing what works — and what doesn't |

---

## What Makes Harmonia Different

Most networks are affiliations — loose membership with no binding commitments. Harmonia is a **trust network**: membership means something, because it requires something.

> [!note] The trust network model
> Members don't just join Harmonia — they pledge to it. Every member, from a small Kenyan NGO to a multinational corporate, signs the same founding pledges and is held to the same standards. This shared accountability is what makes cross-sector collaboration possible.
