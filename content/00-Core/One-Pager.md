# Harmonia: The Trust Network for Collaborative Social Impact

---

## The Problem

Thousands of organisations — NGOs, companies, startups, foundations — are all working on the same global challenges. But they work in silos, duplicate each other's efforts, compete for the same grants, and struggle to find partners they can actually trust.

The sector has more than enough talent, funding, and technology. What it lacks is **trust infrastructure** — a verified, accountable space where genuine cross-sector collaboration can happen.

---

## What Harmonia Is

Harmonia is a **trust network** that unites NGOs, corporates, startups, and philanthropists through shared principles and binding pledges.

Members don't just affiliate — they **commit**. Every member, from a Nairobi grassroots NGO to a London corporate foundation, signs the same pledges and is held to the same standards. This shared accountability is what makes real collaboration possible.

**The result:** A network where the whole is far greater than the sum of its parts.

---

## How It Works

| For NGOs & Charities | For Corporates | For Startups | For Philanthropists |
|---------------------|----------------|--------------|-------------------|
| Vetted partners. Reduced duplication. Collective voice. | Verified implementing partners. Credible impact measurement. | NGO partners. Corporate clients. Philanthropic capital. | Pooled funding. Field intelligence. Leverage. |

All members access: **partner discovery**, **shared measurement**, **peer accountability**, and **cross-sector programming** through the Unified Impact Platform.

---

## Where We're Starting

Harmonia launches with two **co-equal founding chapters** — Kenya and the UK — simultaneously. Each chapter has its own governance, legal entity, and local founding cohort. Both are connected through a shared cross-chapter council with equal representation.

**Founding member target:** 7–12 organisations per chapter (15–25 total).

Founding members help shape the final pledge language and governance before it is ratified. This is an invitation to build, not just to join.

---

## What We're Asking

We are looking for **founding members** who:
- Are genuinely committed to cross-sector collaboration — not just in principle, but in practice
- Are willing to make and be held to a binding pledge
- Want to help design the trust infrastructure the sector needs
