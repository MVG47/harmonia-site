## Kenya and UK as Co-Equal Founding Chapters {#kenya-uk-chapters}

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Date | 2026-04-10 |
| Decided by | Founding team |
| Context | Harmonia required a geographic starting point for Phase 1 operations |

**Decision:** Kenya and the United Kingdom will be established as Harmonia's two co-equal founding chapters, launching simultaneously rather than sequentially.

**Rationale:** A co-equal model demonstrates from the outset that Harmonia is not a Northern-led organisation with a Southern programme — it is a genuinely global trust network in which Global South chapters have equal governance authority. Kenya was selected as the first African chapter because of its strong NGO ecosystem, leading startup hub (Nairobi), and presence of international funders. The UK was selected as the first European chapter because of its large development NGO sector, active social enterprise movement, and concentration of philanthropic capital.

**Implications:** Each chapter will have its own local legal entity, governance committee, and recruitment target (7–12 founding members each). Cross-chapter governance requires a shared council with equal representation. Legal incorporation must proceed in both countries simultaneously.

**Source:** Founding team discussion, 2026-04-10
