## What pledges are

When an organisation joins Harmonia, they don't just sign up — they sign on. Member pledges are the binding commitments that give the trust network its substance. They apply equally to all members regardless of sector, size, or geography.

Pledges are not one-time statements. They are renewed annually, with each renewal accompanied by a reflection on how the previous year's commitments were met.

---

## The Four Pledge Categories

### Pledge 1 — Resource Sharing

> *"I commit to actively contributing resources — time, expertise, networks, funding, or in-kind support — to benefit fellow Harmonia members and shared network initiatives."*

**Minimum commitments:**
- Dedicate a minimum of [X] hours of staff time per year to cross-member collaboration (amount to be set by chapter)
- Respond to resource requests from fellow members within 30 days
- Offer at least one form of in-kind support (pro bono advisory, platform access, venue, etc.) per year when capacity allows

**What counts as a contribution:**
- Technical assistance to another member's project
- Co-hosting or co-facilitating network events
- Opening your networks/contacts to benefit a fellow member
- Contributing to pooled funding rounds
- Hosting site visits or learning exchanges

---

### Pledge 2 — Transparent Reporting

> *"I commit to honest, timely reporting of my organisation's activities, outcomes, and challenges — sharing not just successes but also what hasn't worked."*

**Minimum commitments:**
- Submit an annual impact report to Harmonia by the agreed date each year
- Report on both progress and shortfalls honestly — a sanitised report that hides failure breaches this pledge
- Disclose any conflicts of interest relevant to network partnerships before engaging in them
- Update member profile on the Unified Impact Platform at least twice per year

**What the annual report covers:**
- Key activities and outcomes from the past year
- Progress against commitments made at last annual review
- Challenges encountered and lessons learned
- Commitments for the coming year

---

### Pledge 3 — Active Participation

> *"I commit to being an active contributor to Harmonia — not a passive member who attends events and extracts value without putting in."*

**Minimum commitments:**
- Participate in at least one cross-sector collaborative initiative per year
- Attend or send a delegate to at least one chapter convening per year
- Join at least one working group or thematic community of practice
- Engage with at least three other members per year in substantive collaboration (beyond introductions)

**What counts as active participation:**
- Co-designing or delivering a joint programme
- Contributing to a working group's outputs
- Mentoring or being mentored by another member
- Participating in peer accountability circles
- Contributing content, case studies, or learning to the network knowledge base

---

### Pledge 4 — Cross-Sector Engagement

> *"I commit to actively engaging across sectors — working with members from sectors different from my own, not just with those most familiar to me."*

**Minimum commitments:**
- Initiate or respond to at least one partnership with a member from a different sector each year
- Approach cross-sector partnerships with openness to learning, not just what you can extract
- Participate in at least one cross-sector event or initiative per year

**Why this matters:** Many networks claim cross-sector collaboration but members gravitate to their own sector. This pledge makes cross-sector engagement a specific, measurable obligation.

## Graduated Consequences

Members who consistently fail to honour their pledges face escalating accountability measures. The intent is supportive before punitive — the goal is always to help a member get back on track.

| Level | Trigger | Response |
|-------|---------|----------|
| **1 — Reminder** | Missed deadline or unfulfilled commitment (first occurrence) | Friendly reminder from chapter coordinator; offer of support |
| **2 — Check-in** | Missed deadline (second occurrence) or significant shortfall on annual report | Formal check-in meeting with chapter committee; support plan agreed |
| **3 — Formal Notice** | Persistent non-compliance or failure to engage with support | Written formal notice; 90-day remediation period |
| **4 — Suspension** | Failure to remediate within agreed timeframe | Temporary suspension of network access and benefits; member can appeal |
| **5 — Removal** | Persistent non-compliance or serious breach of principles | Removal from Harmonia; decision made by cross-chapter governance council |

> [!note] Appeal rights
> Members have the right to appeal at Levels 3–5. Appeals are heard by a panel that does not include any member involved in the original decision. See [[Accountability-Mechanisms]] for the full process.

## Signing the Pledges

Founding members sign the pledges as part of their onboarding process. See [[Member-Onboarding-Protocol]] for the full process.

The pledge document is a living instrument — founding members have the opportunity to refine the specific language before it is finalised. Subsequent members sign the ratified version.
