## Overview

Training and advisory services are both a **member benefit** and a **revenue stream** for Harmonia. As the network develops genuine expertise in cross-sector collaboration, partnership brokering, and trust-based accountability, that expertise has external market value — to organisations that are not Harmonia members but want to develop similar practices.

This revenue stream is a Phase 2 development — it requires 12+ months of network operation before Harmonia has the credibility and evidence base to offer it externally.

---

## Member-facing training (Phase 1 onwards)

Included in membership:
- **Partnership brokering basics** — training for member staff in facilitation of cross-sector partnerships, drawing on The Partnering Initiative methodology
- **Impact measurement fundamentals** — how to define a theory of change, set KPIs, and report honestly
- **Peer accountability circle facilitation** — how to run productive peer accountability conversations

Delivered by: Chapter Coordinators (trained), with support from expert members who offer training as a resource contribution.

---

## External training and advisory (Phase 2 onwards)

Paid services for non-members:
- **Cross-sector partnership design workshops** — for organisations wanting to build their own partnership brokering capacity
- **Trust network design consultancy** — for coalitions or networks wanting to add accountability and pledge mechanisms to their structure
- **Impact measurement design** — for organisations wanting to build a credible measurement framework
- **Innovation Hub design** — for sector bodies wanting to replicate the challenge brief / sprint model

Pricing: day-rate consultancy; workshop fees; potential licensing of Harmonia methodology.

---

## Revenue potential

At Phase 3 scale, training and advisory could contribute 15–25% of network revenue — reducing dependence on membership fees alone and creating a financially sustainable model that doesn't rely on continuously growing membership.
