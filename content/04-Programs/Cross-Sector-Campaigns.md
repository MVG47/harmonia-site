## What Cross-Sector Campaigns Are

Cross-sector campaigns are coordinated collective actions — advocacy, public campaigns, or joint service delivery — that involve members from multiple sectors working together under the Harmonia banner. They are the highest-visibility expression of what the trust network can achieve.

Unlike Innovation Hub sprints (which are bounded, internal, and product-focused), campaigns are outward-facing and designed to influence policy, shift public discourse, or deliver coordinated services at scale.

Campaigns launch in Phase 2 at the earliest. They require a mature member base and proven cross-sector relationships before the network has credibility to lead them.

---

## Campaign Types

### Collective advocacy
Members from across sectors jointly advocate to governments or policy-makers on an issue where Harmonia's cross-sector composition gives it unique legitimacy:
- Example: Joint advocacy for stronger corporate accountability on social commitments (NGOs + startups + corporates speaking as one voice carries more weight than NGOs alone)
- Example: Advocacy for trust-based philanthropy practices in national grant-making frameworks

### Joint service delivery
Multiple members co-delivering a programme or service that no single member could deliver alone:
- Example: A fintech startup's platform + an NGO's community reach + a corporate's funding → a financial literacy programme reaching 50,000 people
- Harmonia provides the coordination infrastructure; members deliver

### Public awareness campaigns
Cross-sector campaigns that build public understanding of collaborative social impact:
- Example: A shared data campaign showing what coordinated cross-sector investment achieved in a specific geography

---

## Design Principles

- **Member-led, not Harmonia-led**: Campaigns are proposed and driven by member organisations; Harmonia provides the platform, coordination, and brand
- **Specific and time-bounded**: Vague campaigns that run indefinitely lose momentum; design with clear objectives and an end date
- **Cross-sector minimum**: Any campaign must involve at least 2 different member types from at least 2 sectors
- **Documented outcomes**: All campaigns are logged as collaborative projects and outcomes reported in the annual network impact report
