### Revenue (Year 1)

| Source | Indicative | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Membership fees (15–25 founding members) | £25,000–£60,000 | Average £2,000–£3,000 per member; founding discount applied |
| Philanthropic grants | £30,000–£60,000 | One or two foundation grants in Year 1 |
| Corporate in-kind (platform, venue) | £5,000–£15,000 | Estimated value of in-kind contributions |
| **Total Year 1 revenue** | **£60,000–£135,000** | |

### Year 1 sustainability check

At the lower end (15 founding members, modest grants), revenue may fall slightly short of costs — a £10,000–£20,000 gap is possible. This is acceptable in Year 1 if:
- The founding team is contributing unremunerated time in Year 1
- At least one philanthropic grant is secured before launch
- Corporate in-kind contributions are actively pursued

**Action:** Secure at least £30,000 in grant funding before committing to both coordinator hires.

## Key Financial Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Coordinator hires are too expensive relative to membership income | Hire at 0.8 FTE in Year 1; scale to full-time in Year 2 once income is confirmed |
| Grant funding not secured before launch | Do not hire both coordinators until at least one grant is confirmed |
| Membership fee collection is slow (founding members don't pay on time) | Invoice on pledge signing; 30-day payment terms; no platform access until fee paid |
| Kenya/UK currency exchange volatility | Kenya chapter income and costs in KES; UK in GBP; avoid cross-currency operational exposure |
