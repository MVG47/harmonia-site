## What the Platform Is

The Unified Impact Platform is Harmonia's digital backbone — the tool that makes the trust network visible, trackable, and scalable. Without it, Harmonia is a set of relationships and a pledge document. With it, Harmonia is a verifiable, data-backed infrastructure for cross-sector collaboration.

The platform is not a social network. It is a **collaboration and accountability tool** — it tracks what members commit to, what they actually do, and who they partner with. Access is restricted to verified members.

---

## Core Functions

### 1. Member Directory and Partner Discovery
The primary reason members log in. A searchable, filterable directory of all Harmonia members across all chapters, with:
- Organisation profile (type, sector, thematic focus, geography, size)
- Current commitments and collaboration interests
- Relationship history (previous Harmonia partnerships documented)
- Warm introduction pathway (request an introduction via the platform)

This is **not** a cold outreach directory. Discovery is within a trusted network — every organisation listed has signed the pledges and passed screening.

### 2. Partnership and Collaboration Log
Members record cross-sector partnerships and collaborations directly in the platform:
- Partner name, type, and chapter
- Nature of collaboration (co-delivery, resource sharing, advocacy, etc.)
- Start date, current status, and outcomes (updated over time)

This data feeds directly into KPI 2 (cross-sector partnerships formed) and KPI 6 (pilot projects). It is also the primary source for the annual network impact report.

### 3. Resource Sharing Pool
A lightweight mechanism for members to offer and request resources:
- Post an offer: "We have 20 hours of legal advice available for an NGO working in financial inclusion"
- Post a request: "Looking for a data analyst for a 6-week project on beneficiary tracking"
- Matched and facilitated by Chapter Coordinators

Contributions logged here feed into KPI 4 (resources mobilised through the network).

### 4. Accountability and Pledge Tracking
For each member, a private dashboard tracking:
- Annual report submission status (submitted / pending / overdue)
- Cross-sector participation (initiatives logged vs. minimum commitment)
- Accountability level (current standing — green / amber / red)
- Peer accountability circle members and meeting log

Chapter Coordinators have a view across all their chapter's members. The Cross-Chapter Council sees aggregate data only.

### 5. Annual Report Submission
Members submit their annual impact report (using the template in [[Reporting-Templates]]) directly through the platform. The report is reviewed by the Chapter Coordinator and relevant sections shared with the member's peer accountability circle.

### 6. Cross-Chapter Discovery
Members can search and connect with members in other chapters — enabling the cross-chapter partnerships tracked in KPI 9. Cross-chapter introductions are facilitated by the relevant Chapter Coordinators.

## MVP vs. Full Platform

**Phase 1 MVP (minimum viable):**
The MVP must be live by Month 4–5 of Phase 1. It does not need to be custom-built. An MVP can be assembled from:
- A simple member directory (Notion database, Airtable, or lightweight custom build)
- A shared workspace for partnership logging (Airtable or similar)
- A form-based annual report submission (Typeform or equivalent)
- A basic dashboard for Chapter Coordinators (Google Sheets or Airtable views)

Do not delay founding member recruitment waiting for a polished platform. The MVP needs to be functional, not beautiful.

**Full platform (Phase 2):**
By Year 2, invest in a properly designed platform that:
- Has a genuine partner discovery UX (search, filter, warm introduction request)
- Integrates collaboration logging with impact measurement
- Provides member-facing dashboards (not just coordinator-facing)
- Enables cross-chapter functionality seamlessly

---

## Platform Principles

- **Member data belongs to members** — Harmonia does not sell or share member data outside the network
- **Transparency by default** — members can see each other's profiles and (summary) partnership activity; detailed accountability data is private
- **Low friction for contribution** — if logging a resource contribution takes more than 3 minutes, members won't do it. Design for the minimum viable input.
- **Trust, not surveillance** — the platform makes commitments visible, not monitored. The goal is accountability through transparency, not tracking through compliance.
