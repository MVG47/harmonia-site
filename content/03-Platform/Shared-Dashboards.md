## Overview

Shared dashboards give members and governance bodies visibility into the network's health — who's collaborating, what resources are being shared, and whether the network is meeting its collective targets. Transparency is a core principle; the dashboards make it structural.

---

## Dashboard Types

### Member dashboard (individual)
Each member sees their own:
- Pledge compliance status (annual report submitted? cross-sector initiative logged? peer circle engaged?)
- Partnerships logged this year
- Resources contributed and received
- Upcoming accountability milestones (report due date, renewal date)

Private to the individual member and Chapter Coordinator.

### Chapter dashboard (Coordinator view)
Chapter Coordinator sees:
- Full chapter member list with compliance status (green / amber / red)
- Chapter KPI progress vs. targets (partnerships, resources, pilot projects)
- Members at risk of accountability escalation
- Upcoming renewals and report deadlines

Shared with the Chapter Committee (aggregated, not individual member detail).

### Network dashboard (Cross-Chapter Council view)
The Cross-Chapter Council sees:
- Network-wide KPI scorecard (all 10 KPIs from [[KPIs-and-Metrics]])
- Chapter-by-chapter comparison
- Cross-chapter partnership count
- Financial sustainability ratio

This view is published annually as the network impact report summary (anonymised where appropriate).

---

## MVP Implementation

Phase 1 MVP dashboards can be built in Google Sheets or Airtable — no custom development required. The member-facing view can be a simple automated email summary rather than a live dashboard.
