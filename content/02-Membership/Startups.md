## Who this is for

Harmonia's startup membership is for social enterprises, impact-driven startups, and technology ventures whose work directly addresses social or environmental challenges. The product or service must be the mechanism of impact — not a side project or future plan.

We particularly welcome startups from Kenya and the UK's tech-for-good ecosystems, where innovation is meeting real social need. Both early-stage and growth-stage startups are welcome; what matters is genuine impact at the core of what you do.

---

## What Startups Bring to Harmonia

Startups are the network's engine of innovation. They bring what neither NGOs nor corporates can:

- **Disruptive solutions** — fresh approaches to problems the sector has been working on for decades
- **Agility** — ability to test, iterate, and adapt faster than large institutions
- **Technology** — digital products, platforms, and data capabilities that multiply what other members can do
- **Fresh perspectives** — no institutional inertia; willingness to challenge assumptions
- **Scalability** — solutions built to grow, not just to deliver in a single context

## What Harmonia Offers Startups

| Benefit | What it means in practice |
|---------|--------------------------|
| **Access to implementing partners** | Direct connections with vetted NGOs who need exactly what you're building — no cold B2B sales cycle |
| **Corporate pathways** | Introductions to corporate members as potential clients, co-development partners, and investors |
| **Philanthropic capital** | Visibility to philanthropist members looking for innovative approaches to fund |
| **Field credibility** | Membership alongside established NGOs signals that your solution is vetted and mission-aligned — valuable for grant applications and investor due diligence |
| **Learning network** | Access to the operational knowledge of NGOs who've been delivering in your target communities for years |
| **Pilot opportunities** | Cross-sector convenings and innovation programming create structured pathways to pilot your solution |

## Admission Criteria

Startup applicants must demonstrate:

1. **Social impact at the core** — the social or environmental mission is intrinsic to the business model, not a marketing layer. Impact-washing is disqualifying.
2. **Working product or service** — at minimum, a pilot or MVP that has been tested with real users. Pre-product concepts are not eligible.
3. **At least 12 months of operation** — enough track record to assess whether the impact claim is real
4. **Founder engagement** — the founder or a senior leader is personally involved in Harmonia, not delegated to a junior team member

> [!note] Early-stage startups
> If you are pre-revenue or early-stage but your product is live and your mission is genuine, the Chapter Committee will consider your application. Fee subsidies are available for qualifying early-stage members. Talk to the Chapter Coordinator.

## What We Ask of Startup Members

In addition to the standard [[Member-Pledges]], startup members are specifically asked to:

- **Share learning openly** — what's working in your solution, what isn't, and what the sector needs to know. Your learning has value beyond your own scaling.
- **Engage with NGO members as real partners** — not just as validation or distribution channels. Co-design with communities, not just for them.
- **Contribute to the innovation agenda** — participate in the Innovation Hub programming, bring problems to the network, and help solve others' problems too.

---

## The Innovation Hub Connection

Harmonia's [[Innovation-Hub]] is the primary programming space for startups within the network. It connects startups with NGO implementation partners and corporate clients in structured formats:

- **Challenge briefs** — NGOs post specific technology or capability needs; startups pitch solutions
- **Co-development sprints** — structured short-term collaborations between startups, NGOs, and corporates
- **Demo days** — internal showcases for startups to present to potential partners and funders within the network

Full Innovation Hub participation is a benefit included in startup membership.
