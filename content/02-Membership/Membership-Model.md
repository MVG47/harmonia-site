## Overview

Harmonia's membership model is built around one central idea: all members are equal in their obligations, regardless of size, sector, or what they contribute financially. There are no tiers that create first- and second-class members.

What varies is the **form** of contribution (a startup offers different resources than a foundation), and the **fee level** (scaled to organisational size so the network is accessible). Everything else — governance rights, platform access, accountability standards — is the same.

---

## Who Can Join

Harmonia welcomes organisations and individuals across four categories:

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **NGOs & Charities** | Registered non-governmental or charitable organisations with a social mission — from local grassroots groups to large international bodies |
| **Corporates** | Companies committed to social good through their core business model or CSR strategy — not as an add-on, but as genuine strategic priority |
| **Startups** | Social enterprises, impact-driven startups, and technology ventures whose products or services directly address social or environmental challenges |
| **Philanthropists** | Individual philanthropists, family offices, and foundations that fund or co-fund social impact work — including collaborative and trust-based givers |

Organisations must demonstrate genuine alignment with Harmonia's [[Core-Principles]] to be admitted. Size is not a criterion — a 3-person Kenyan NGO has the same right to membership as a global INGO.

---

## Membership Rights (all members)

Regardless of type or fee level, every member receives:

- Full voting rights (one member, one vote) in chapter governance
- Access to the Unified Impact Platform — partner discovery, shared dashboards, resource pool
- Eligibility to stand for Chapter Committee election
- Access to the full member directory and relationship map
- Participation in working groups and thematic communities of practice
- Invitation to all chapter convenings and the annual cross-chapter gathering
- Right to raise proposals to the Chapter Committee and Cross-Chapter Council
- Access to peer accountability circle support

---

## Membership Obligations (all members)

Every member, regardless of type, commits to the four [[Member-Pledges]]:

1. **Resource Sharing** — actively contributing time, expertise, networks, or in-kind support
2. **Transparent Reporting** — annual impact report, honest about successes and shortfalls
3. **Active Participation** — at least one cross-sector initiative per year, convening attendance
4. **Cross-Sector Engagement** — at least one documented partnership with a different-sector member per year

## Membership Fees

Fees are scaled to organisational size and type. The principle: organisations with greater financial resources pay more, so that the network remains accessible to grassroots and early-stage members who bring irreplaceable implementation knowledge and community trust.

> [!note] Indicative fee structure (to be ratified by founding members)
> The specific fee bands below are indicative and will be confirmed by the founding cohort before the network launches. Both chapter currencies (GBP and KES) apply — fees are set in local currency by each chapter.

| Band | Criteria | Annual fee (indicative) |
|------|----------|------------------------|
| **Grassroots** | NGO/charity with annual income <£100k / <KES 15M | Subsidised / by arrangement |
| **Small** | Organisation with annual income £100k–£500k / KES 15M–75M | £500–£1,500 / KES 75k–225k |
| **Medium** | Organisation with annual income £500k–£5M / KES 75M–750M | £1,500–£5,000 / KES 225k–750k |
| **Large** | Organisation with annual income >£5M / KES >750M | £5,000–£15,000 / KES 750k–2.2M |
| **Corporate** | Commercial companies | £5,000–£25,000 / KES 750k–3.75M (based on size and CSR budget) |
| **Philanthropist** | Individual / family office | £2,000–£10,000 / KES 300k–1.5M |

In-kind contributions can substitute for a portion of fees for Grassroots members, subject to Chapter Committee approval.

**Founding member discount:** Founding members (Phase 1 cohort) receive a 25% discount on membership fees in Year 1, in recognition of the higher commitment required to help shape the network.

## Chapter Balance Requirements

Each chapter aims to maintain a balanced membership across sectors:

| Type | Target share of chapter membership |
|------|------------------------------------|
| NGOs & Charities | 35–45% |
| Corporates | 20–30% |
| Startups | 20–30% |
| Philanthropists | 10–20% |

These are targets, not hard caps. The goal is to prevent any single sector from dominating the network's culture or agenda.

---

## Membership Renewal

Membership renews annually. The renewal process:
1. Member submits annual impact report (by 31 March)
2. Member confirms continued alignment with pledges via a renewal declaration
3. Fee payment for the coming year
4. Chapter Committee confirms renewal (routine — only flagged if compliance concerns exist)
