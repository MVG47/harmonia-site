## Overview

Phase 1 is the year in which Harmonia becomes real — moving from concept and documentation to a living network with founding members, ratified governance, and an operational platform. It runs from network launch through to the end of Year 1.

**Target outcome:** A trust network with 15–25 founding members across two co-equal chapters (Kenya and UK), ratified governance, active member relationships, and at least 2 pilot cross-sector projects underway.

---

## Chapter Targets

| Chapter | Founding members target | Sector balance |
|---------|------------------------|----------------|
| **Kenya** | 7–12 organisations | Min. 2 NGOs, 2 startups, 2 corporates, 1 philanthropist |
| **UK** | 7–12 organisations | Min. 2 NGOs, 2 startups, 2 corporates, 1 philanthropist |

Founding members are not just the first to join — they shape the final form of the pledges, governance, and platform. Founding membership closes when each chapter reaches its target cohort.

## Month-by-Month Milestones

### Months 1–2: Legal and Governance Foundation

- [ ] Instruct solicitors in UK to register Harmonia as a Community Interest Company (CIC) — target: Companies House registration complete within 6 weeks
- [ ] Instruct advocate in Nairobi to register Kenya chapter as a Company Limited by Guarantee — target: Companies Registry filing within 6 weeks
- [ ] Ratify Governance Framework with founding team
- [ ] Appoint interim Chapter Coordinators for both chapters (paid roles)
- [ ] Open founding member application pipeline in both geographies
- [ ] Complete `01-Governance/` vault documentation

### Months 2–3: Founding Member Recruitment — Round 1

- [ ] Target: 5+ founding member applications received per chapter
- [ ] Launch outreach to anchor NGOs in both geographies (UK: Bond network contacts; Kenya: NGO umbrella bodies, GrowthAfrica)
- [ ] Conduct introductory meetings with top 10 prospects per chapter
- [ ] Review first applications at Chapter Committee meetings (interim committee composed of founding team + early adopters)
- [ ] First founding members sign pledges

### Months 3–4: Founding Member Recruitment — Round 2

- [ ] Target: chapters at 60–70% of founding member targets
- [ ] First cross-chapter virtual convening — founding members from both chapters meet each other
- [ ] Workshop 1: Refine pledge language with founding members
- [ ] Workshop 2: Review and ratify Governance Framework with founding members
- [ ] Elect first Chapter Committees (from founding member cohort)

### Months 4–6: Platform and Impact Framework

- [ ] Launch Unified Impact Platform (MVP) — partner discovery, member directory, resource log
- [ ] All founding members complete platform profiles
- [ ] Publish network-level impact framework and KPI definitions
- [ ] Launch peer accountability circles (group founding members into circles of 4–6 across sectors)
- [ ] Pilot cross-sector initiative 1: identify via founding member interest mapping

### Months 6–9: First Cross-Sector Projects

- [ ] Target: at least 2 pilot cross-sector projects formally underway (one per chapter or one joint)
- [ ] First Cross-Chapter Council meeting (elected committees from both chapters)
- [ ] Founding Council formally transfers authority to Cross-Chapter Council (as per Governance Framework sunset)
- [ ] Launch Innovation Hub pilot programming (connecting startups with NGO partners on a specific challenge brief)
- [ ] Mid-year check-in with all founding members on pledge commitments

### Months 9–12: Consolidation and Phase 2 Preparation

- [ ] All founding members submit first impact reports (in preparation for the formal annual cycle)
- [ ] First annual governance review published by Cross-Chapter Council
- [ ] Recruitment pipeline opens for Phase 2 (non-founding) members
- [ ] Financial review: first-year revenues vs. costs, phase 2 budget agreed
- [ ] Begin outreach for next expansion geography (Phase 2 regional hub candidate)
- [ ] Full network convening: all members, both chapters (in-person if possible)

## Decision Gates

Before moving to Phase 2, the network must have achieved:

| Gate | Criteria |
|------|----------|
| **Membership** | 15+ founding members active across both chapters |
| **Governance** | Cross-Chapter Council elected and meeting; Founding Council dissolved |
| **Pledges** | Pledge language ratified by founding members |
| **Legal** | Both chapter legal entities formally registered |
| **Platform** | MVP live with >80% of founding members actively using it |
| **Projects** | At least 2 pilot cross-sector projects with documented early outcomes |
| **Finance** | 12-month financial position reviewed; Phase 2 funding plan in place |

---

## Key Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Founding member recruitment slower than expected | Start with warm contacts; prioritise quality over speed; a smaller founding cohort of 10–15 is better than rushing to 25 with ill-fitting members |
| Legal incorporation delays (especially Kenya) | Begin legal processes in Month 1; have a Memorandum of Association ready; use CLG as default if NGO registration is delayed |
| Sector imbalance in founding cohort | Monitor applications by sector; if one sector is underrepresented, actively target via sector-specific entry points |
| Founding Council reluctant to hand over authority | The sunset is explicit and non-negotiable in the Governance Framework — no ambiguity |
| Platform MVP not ready | Prioritise platform readiness for Month 4–5; use lightweight interim tools (shared workspace) if needed |
