## Goal

Recruit 7–12 founding members for the Kenya chapter across all four member types, with a minimum of: 2 NGOs, 2 startups, 2 corporates, and 1 philanthropist/funder.

Founding members are not just the first to join — they co-shape Harmonia's pledge language and governance before it is finalised. The pitch should reflect this: we are not asking them to join something fully built. We are inviting them to build it with us.

---

## Approach: Ecosystem First, Cold Outreach Last

Kenya's social impact ecosystem is well-networked — organisations know each other, and trust travels through relationships. Cold outreach from an unknown network will underperform. The strategy is:

1. **Establish one or two anchor relationships** with credible intermediary organisations (GrowthAfrica, Impact Hub Nairobi, Kenya NGO Council)
2. **Soft launch event** co-hosted or hosted by an anchor intermediary — introduce Harmonia to 30–50 relevant organisations in one room
3. **Follow up with warm prospects** from the event and any referrals from the anchor intermediary
4. **Direct outreach** to specifically targeted organisations that didn't attend the event

---

## Phase 1: Anchor Relationships (Month 1)

Before any member outreach, establish a relationship with at least one ecosystem intermediary. These organisations have trust that Harmonia needs to borrow while it builds its own.

| Intermediary | Why them | What to propose |
|-------------|----------|-----------------|
| **GrowthAfrica** | Pan-African accelerator; credibility with both startups and impact-focused NGOs; Nairobi-based | Co-host a launch event; explore formal partnership for startup member pipeline |
| **Impact Hub Nairobi** | Global Impact Hub chapter; mixed-sector community; strong cross-sector convening history | Co-host or venue partner for launch event; explore member referral relationship |
| **Kenya NGO Council** | Umbrella body for Kenya's registered NGOs; access to the broadest NGO audience | Endorsement or official introduction to their member newsletter/events |
| **KEPSA (Kenya Private Sector Alliance)** | Premier private sector body; direct access to corporate ESG leads | Introduction to social impact committee members; co-design outreach to corporate prospects |

---

## Phase 2: Soft Launch Event (Month 2)

A half-day or evening event in Nairobi introducing Harmonia to prospective founding members.

**Format:** Panel/discussion on "Why cross-sector collaboration fails — and what a trust network could do differently", followed by a reception and structured conversations between attendees.

**Target attendees:** 30–50 individuals from NGOs, startups, corporates, and funders who are already interested in cross-sector collaboration. Quality over quantity — this is not a general sector event.

**Desired outcome:** 15+ warm prospect conversations; 5+ expressions of founding member interest on the night.

**Hosting:** Co-hosted with GrowthAfrica or Impact Hub Nairobi (borrowing their credibility and network).

---

## Phase 3: Prospect Outreach by Sector

### NGO / Charity Prospects

**Criteria:** Active programming in at least one Kenya priority theme; transparent (willing to share annual report); genuine cross-sector appetite (not NGO-purist); leadership that can make the pledge commitment credible.

**Priority prospect categories:**
- An international NGO with a strong Kenya office that is already doing private sector partnerships (e.g. Aga Khan Foundation Kenya, Practical Action East Africa)
- A well-respected local NGO in health or agriculture — credibility with grassroots communities is what the network needs
- A mid-size NGO in financial inclusion or civic tech — likely to value startup partnerships

**Pitch emphasis:** Partner discovery and reduced duplication; collective voice; access to corporate and startup resources they currently can't access individually.

---

### Startup Prospects

**Criteria:** Social impact at the core (not a side project); working product or service; operating for at least 12 months; founder or senior leader personally engaged.

**Priority sectors and types:**
- **Agritech:** companies working directly with smallholder farmers (Apollo Agriculture, similar stage companies)
- **Fintech / Financial inclusion:** companies focused on underserved communities (M-KOPA at the larger end; earlier-stage fintechs building on M-Pesa infrastructure)
- **Healthtech:** digital health platforms serving public health system needs (diagnostic tools, community health worker platforms)
- **Climate:** clean energy or climate adaptation startups with demonstrable community impact

**Entry pathway:** Approach via GrowthAfrica's portfolio and event network; iHub community; Antler Kenya cohort alumni.

**Pitch emphasis:** NGO partner access and field credibility; philanthropic capital visibility; corporate client introductions — all things that typically require years of relationship-building but come immediately with founding membership.

---

### Corporate Prospects

**Criteria:** Dedicated social impact or ESG team (not just marketing); senior leadership willing to personally engage; no conflicts of interest with NGO members; willing to contribute genuinely (expertise, platforms, secondments) not just fund.

**Priority organisations:**
- **Safaricom** — M-Pesa Foundation; some of the most active CSR in Kenya; strong alignment with financial inclusion and digital themes
- **KCB Group** — KCB Foundation; banking and financial inclusion; active East Africa footprint
- **Equity Bank** — Equity Group Foundation; particularly active in education and entrepreneurship support
- **Absa Kenya** — active CSR and ESG programme; aligned with financial inclusion
- **East African Breweries / Diageo** — track record of cross-sector social investment; large Kenya footprint

**Entry pathway:** KEPSA social impact committee; direct outreach to Foundation Directors / Head of CSR; warm introductions via NGO members who already have corporate relationships.

**Pitch emphasis:** Vetted NGO and startup partners; impact measurement credibility; collective advocacy access; workforce engagement opportunities.

---

### Philanthropist / Funder Prospects

**Criteria:** Active grant-making in Kenya or East Africa; interest in collaborative approaches; programme officer or foundation director who will personally engage (not a passive board-level relationship).

**Priority organisations:**
- **Mastercard Foundation** — one of the largest funders in Kenya; specific programmes in education and financial inclusion; Nairobi office
- **Ford Foundation** — Nairobi regional office; active in economic justice and civic society
- **British Council Kenya** — cultural/educational funding; convening power
- **Open Society East Africa** — governance, civil society, justice; open to trust-based approaches

**Entry pathway:** These are relationship organisations — approach through any existing connections first; alternatively, through NGO members who are already grantees.

**Pitch emphasis:** Pooled funding mechanisms; reduced due diligence burden through verified member vetting; real-time field intelligence from NGO and startup members; leverage effect (Harmonia membership multiplies the impact of any single grant).
