## Overview

Phase 2 runs across Years 2–3 of Harmonia's development. Building on the trust and infrastructure established in Phase 1, this phase focuses on membership expansion, the launch of the Innovation Hub as a formal programme, regional coordination deepening, and the development of shared measurement and accountability systems that can scale beyond the founding cohort.

**Entering Phase 2 requires:** Completion of all Phase 1 decision gates (see [[Phase-1-Foundation]]). Do not begin Phase 2 expansion before founding members are stable, governance is ratified, and at least 2 pilot cross-sector projects are underway.

---

## Phase 2 Targets

| Metric | End of Phase 2 target |
|--------|----------------------|
| Total active members | 50–80 across Kenya and UK |
| Chapters | 2 founding + 1 new chapter approved (Phase 3 launch) |
| Cross-sector partnerships documented | 40+ network-wide |
| Pilot projects completed | 5+ with documented outcomes |
| Innovation Hub cohorts run | 2 (1 per chapter or 1 joint) |
| Pledge compliance rate | 85%+ |
| Network financial sustainability | 80%+ of operating costs covered by membership fees and earned revenue |

---

## Year 2 Priorities

### Membership expansion
- Open applications to non-founding members in both chapters
- Introduce differentiated onboarding for non-founding members (pledge language now finalised)
- Target: 15–20 new members per chapter across Year 2
- Maintain sector balance targets during expansion — actively manage the pipeline if any sector is underrepresented

### Innovation Hub launch
- Design and run the first Innovation Hub cohort in each chapter
- Format: challenge brief from an NGO member → startup pitches solutions → structured 90-day co-development sprint → corporate mentor/funder engagement
- Outcome: at least 1 viable pilot per cohort; documented as a cross-sector project for network KPIs
- Consider a joint Kenya/UK cohort addressing a shared thematic challenge (e.g. climate adaptation tech)

### Shared measurement systems
- All members now using the Unified Impact Platform for partnership logging and resource sharing
- First full annual reporting cycle complete (reports submitted by 31 March Year 2)
- Publish first network-level impact report (all-member transparency)
- Refine KPIs and measurement methods based on Year 1 data quality

### Accountability system maturity
- Peer accountability circles active and functioning independently (not just Chapter Coordinator-managed)
- First Level 3 cases (if any) handled through the formal process; lessons documented and shared with the Cross-Chapter Council
- Annual governance review published

### Regional coordination
- Kenya chapter: establish connections to East African ecosystem players (Uganda, Tanzania, Ethiopia) in preparation for regional hub concept
- UK chapter: establish connections to European development and social enterprise networks (for potential European chapter in Phase 3)

---

## Year 3 Priorities

### Membership deepening (quality over quantity)
- Retention focus: ensure founding members and Year 2 cohort are deeply engaged, not just enrolled
- Introduce member peer review: members review each other's annual reports and flag learning opportunities (not just accountability)
- First annual member survey to measure network satisfaction and collaboration depth

### Third chapter preparation
- Identify the strongest candidate geography for Chapter 3 based on: ecosystem readiness, existing relationships, thematic alignment, and cross-chapter demand
- Candidate geographies to assess: West Africa (Ghana or Nigeria), South/Southeast Asia, or a European hub
- Conduct feasibility assessment; bring to Cross-Chapter Council for approval by end of Year 3

### Sustainability revenue growth
- Launch the first paid Training and Advisory offering for external organisations (using Harmonia's partnership brokering methodology, informed by The Partnering Initiative's approach)
- Explore a philanthropic pooled fund mechanism for philanthropist members — enabling collaborative grant-making to NGO and startup members
- Begin earned revenue generating activities to reduce grant dependency

### Platform evolution
- Unified Impact Platform v2 based on Year 1–2 user feedback
- Cross-chapter partner discovery working reliably
- Resource-sharing log standardised and being used consistently

## Key Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Expansion dilutes network quality | Maintain rigorous screening; sector balance actively managed; peer review of applications from Year 2 onwards |
| Innovation Hub fails to produce viable partnerships | Design with small, bounded sprints first; document and learn from Year 1 cohort before scaling Year 2 |
| Founding members disengage as new members join | Create a founding member "alumni" identity; ensure founding members have priority in Innovation Hub facilitation roles |
| Third chapter geography chosen poorly | Rigorous feasibility assessment; Cross-Chapter Council must approve; existing members should have connections in the target geography |
