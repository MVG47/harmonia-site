> [!note] Scope
> This process covers two distinct situations: (1) a member disputing a pledge compliance decision against them, and (2) disputes between members. It is separate from the [[Accountability-Mechanisms]] graduated response ladder, which applies to clear pledge violations.

---

## Principles

All conflict resolution at Harmonia follows these principles:

- **Proportionality** — the process used should be proportionate to the seriousness of the dispute
- **Confidentiality** — parties agree not to disclose proceedings outside the process without consent
- **Good faith** — all parties engage honestly and with a genuine desire to resolve the issue
- **Coordinator as neutral facilitator** — not advocate for either party
- **Network stability** — resolution should preserve the member's ability to remain in the network where possible

---

## Type 1: Dispute of a Pledge Compliance Decision

A member who has received a Formal Notice or Suspension under the [[Accountability-Mechanisms]] ladder may invoke this process to dispute:

- The factual basis of the allegation (they deny the pledge breach occurred)
- The proportionality of the response (they accept there was an issue but dispute the severity)
- A procedural error in the graduated response process

### How to invoke

The member submits a written dispute to the Chapter Coordinator within **14 days** of receiving the Formal Notice or Suspension decision. The written dispute must:

1. State clearly what is being disputed (factual basis, proportionality, or procedure)
2. Provide any supporting evidence
3. Propose a resolution

### Review panel

A three-person review panel is convened:

| Seat | Holder |
|------|--------|
| Independent Chair | A member of the Chapter Committee not involved in the original decision |
| Member representative | Chosen by the disputing member from the active member pool |
| Coordinator | Chapter Coordinator (non-voting; facilitates) |

### Process

1. The panel reviews the written dispute and the original decision documentation
2. Both the disputing member and the Chapter Coordinator (on behalf of the committee) may submit written statements
3. An oral hearing may be requested by either party — the panel decides whether to grant it
4. The panel issues a written decision within **21 days** of the dispute being submitted

### Panel decisions

The panel may:

- **Uphold the dispute fully** — the original compliance decision is reversed; the member's record is cleared
- **Uphold the dispute in part** — the sanction is reduced (e.g. from Suspension to Formal Notice)
- **Dismiss the dispute** — the original decision stands

Panel decisions are final at chapter level. A member may escalate to the Cross-Chapter Council only on grounds of procedural fairness, not on the merits of the original decision.

## Type 2: Disputes Between Members

Disputes between members — over partnership commitments, resource sharing, conduct, or other matters — are handled separately from pledge compliance.

### Step 1: Direct resolution (7 days)

The parties attempt to resolve the matter directly. The Chapter Coordinator may offer to facilitate an informal conversation.

### Step 2: Mediation (14 days)

If direct resolution fails, either party may request mediation. The Chapter Coordinator acts as mediator. Mediation is:

- Voluntary — both parties must agree
- Confidential — no record enters the accountability system unless a pledge breach is separately identified
- Non-binding — the mediator facilitates agreement but cannot impose an outcome

### Step 3: Formal adjudication (21 days)

If mediation fails, either party may request formal adjudication by the Chapter Committee. The committee:

1. Hears both parties (written submissions; oral hearing optional)
2. Issues a written finding within 21 days
3. May determine that one or more parties has committed a pledge breach, triggering the [[Accountability-Mechanisms]] ladder from the appropriate level

### Situations that cannot be adjudicated

The Chapter Committee does not adjudicate on:

- Commercial disputes between members (refer to appropriate legal channels)
- Disputes predating membership
- Matters involving criminal allegations (refer to appropriate authorities)

---

## Cross-Chapter Disputes

Where a dispute involves members from different chapters, the Cross-Chapter Council appoints a joint review panel with equal representation from both chapter committees. The process mirrors Type 1 or Type 2 above as appropriate.
