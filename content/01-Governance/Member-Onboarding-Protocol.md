## Purpose

This protocol defines the journey from prospective member to active Harmonia member — the steps, responsibilities, and experience we want every new member to have. Onboarding is where the trust network begins. Getting it right matters.

## Scope

Applies to all prospective members in both the Kenya and UK chapters, and any future chapters. Founding members (Phase 1) follow a slightly modified process noted below.

---

## The Member Journey

```
Prospect → Application → Review → Pledge Signing → Welcome → Active Member
```

---

## Stage 1: Prospect

**Who:** Organisations that have expressed interest, been referred by an existing member, or been identified through outreach.

**What happens:**
- Prospect receives the Harmonia Concept Note and One-Pager
- Introductory call or meeting with the Chapter Coordinator (30–45 min)
- Prospect is directed to the member information pack (overview of pledges, benefits, and what membership involves)

**Chapter Committee role:** None yet — this stage is managed by the Chapter Coordinator.

**Duration:** No time limit — prospects move to application when they are ready.

> [!note] Founding members (Phase 1)
> During Phase 1, the Founding Council and Chapter Coordinator manage prospect conversations directly. The first 7–12 members per chapter are founding members and help shape the pledge language before it is finalised.

---

## Stage 2: Application

**What the applicant submits:**
1. Completed membership application form (available from Chapter Coordinator)
2. Organisation overview: mission, programmes, key stakeholders, annual report (or equivalent)
3. Brief statement (300–500 words) on why they want to join Harmonia and what they commit to bringing
4. Declaration of any conflicts of interest with current Harmonia members

**Who reviews:** The Chapter Committee reviews all applications.

**Timeline:** Applications are reviewed at the next Chapter Committee meeting (meetings are at least monthly). Applicants are notified of the outcome within 5 working days of that meeting.

---

## Stage 3: Review and Decision

The Chapter Committee assesses each application against three criteria:

| Criterion | What we look for |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Mission alignment** | Genuine commitment to social good — not performative CSR or reputational positioning |
| **Pledge readiness** | Realistic capacity to fulfil the four pledge categories; smaller organisations assessed proportionally |
| **Sector balance** | Does admitting this member maintain or improve the chapter's cross-sector balance? |

**Decision outcomes:**
- **Approved**: Proceed to Stage 4
- **Approved with conditions**: Specific commitments or a probationary review after 6 months
- **Deferred**: More information needed — Committee requests within 14 days; applicant resubmits within 30 days
- **Declined**: Clear communication of the reason; applicant may reapply after 12 months

> [!important] Declined applications
> A decline is not a judgement on the organisation's quality — it may reflect sector balance, timing, or capacity concerns. The Chapter Coordinator communicates declines with care and, where possible, with a pathway for future engagement.

Decisions require a simple majority of the Chapter Committee. A member with a conflict of interest recuses from the discussion and vote.

---

## Stage 4: Pledge Signing

**What happens:**
- The applicant receives the Member Agreement (see [[Member-Onboarding-Protocol#member-agreement]]) and the current Member Pledges document
- A 7-day review period — applicants are encouraged to discuss the pledges internally and ask questions
- Pledge signing meeting (video or in-person) with the Chapter Coordinator and at least one Committee member present
- Both parties sign the Member Agreement (digital signature accepted)
- The signed agreement is stored securely by the chapter

**What is signed:**
- The Member Agreement (the binding legal document)
- A personal letter of commitment from the organisation's senior leader (encouraged but not required — this is the human document alongside the legal one)

> [!note] Founding members
> Founding members sign a version of the pledges that is explicitly marked as "founding draft" — they are co-creating the final language. Once the founding cohort ratifies the final pledge text, they re-sign or confirm acceptance of the final version.

---

## Stage 5: Welcome

The first impression of membership sets the tone for everything that follows.

**Within 48 hours of signing:**
- Welcome message from the Chapter Coordinator and a Committee member
- Access credentials for the Unified Impact Platform
- Introduction to their peer accountability circle (3–5 other members)

**Within the first month:**
- One-to-one welcome meeting with a Chapter Committee member (30 min) — learn about the new member's priorities and identify early collaboration opportunities
- Introduction email to the full chapter membership
- Briefing on: upcoming events, active working groups, and how to log resource sharing on the platform

**Within the first 3 months:**
- Participation in at least one network event or working group
- Completion of the member profile on the platform
- First check-in with their peer accountability circle

---

## Stage 6: Active Membership

After 3 months, members transition from "new member" to "active member" status. This is not a formal gate — it simply marks the end of the structured onboarding period.

**Active member responsibilities:**
- Full compliance with pledge commitments
- Participation in the annual impact reporting cycle
- Engagement with their peer accountability circle
- Attendance (or delegation) at chapter convenings

**Annual renewal:**
- Membership is renewed annually, confirmed by submission of the annual impact report and a brief renewal declaration
- Members who do not renew by the deadline are contacted — non-response leads to a Level 1 accountability reminder

---

## Member Agreement {#member-agreement}

The Member Agreement is the legal document all members sign. It covers:
- Membership obligations (the four pledge categories)
- Data sharing and confidentiality
- Intellectual property (content contributed to the platform)
- Dispute resolution agreement
- Exit terms (how a member leaves voluntarily)
- Governing law (UK law for UK chapter; Kenyan law for Kenya chapter)

> [!warning] Requires legal drafting
> The Member Agreement must be drafted by solicitors in each country and reviewed before any member signs. The governance framework and pledge documents provide the substance — legal counsel provides the legally enforceable wrapper.

---

## Offboarding (Voluntary Exit)

Members who wish to leave Harmonia:
1. Notify the Chapter Coordinator in writing (30 days' notice)
2. Complete any in-progress commitments to fellow members or initiatives (transition period agreed case by case)
3. Platform access is removed on the exit date
4. The member's profile is archived (not deleted) — they remain in the network's history

Members who leave in good standing may rejoin without waiting period. Members who left under Level 3+ accountability review follow the re-application timeline defined in [[Accountability-Mechanisms]].

---

## Review and Amendment

- **Review cycle**: Annually, and after each significant onboarding cohort
- **Amendment process**: Chapter Committee approval (operational details); Cross-Chapter Council approval (standards that apply network-wide)
- **Owner**: Chapter Committees, with Cross-Chapter Council oversight
